What it does

Everything you would do yourself, done the same way a player does it: with the mouse and the keyboard.

📈

Level progression 1 → 60

Level missions and pirate trials in the right order: it knows who gives each mission, where to go, what to sink or collect and where to hand it in.

📜

Daily missions

Tick the daily missions you want; it takes them from the contractors, completes them and hands them in, one after another.

🗺

Bonus maps

Make your own list — which maps, how many of each and in what order. It enters, clears every wave and moves to the next one.

⚔

Smart combat

Picks the closest target, closes in when it is out of range, switches target when the current one is gone and never wastes time on a ship it cannot hit.

🧰

Chests & glitter

Collects the chests and glitters you choose, including the special ones your missions ask for.

🧪

Items when you need them

Uses your items automatically — in fights, while sailing or only on bonus maps — with the interval you set.

⚓

Repairs & recovery

Repairs when needed, comes back after being sunk, logs back in after a disconnect and closes the pop-ups that get in the way.

🖥

Any screen, any account

Works with any window size, zoom and layout of the game windows, on any account. English and Romanian panel.

🔄

Always up to date

New versions install themselves when you start the program. Problems are reported automatically so they get fixed fast (you can turn this off).

Install in 2 minutes

No installer, nothing to configure in Windows.

  1. Download the zip with the button above and extract it anywhere (for example on your Desktop).
  2. Open the “Kraken Bot” folder and start Kraken Bot.exe.
  3. Log in to Seafight in the window that opens, as you normally do in your browser.
  4. In the panel, write your ship name (Settings), pick what the bot should do (Home) and press START.

FAQ

How does it play?

Like a player sitting at the computer: it clicks and presses keys in the game window. It does not change the game and does not send anything to the server by itself.

Does it need my password?

No. You log in yourself, in the program's own window. The bot never reads or stores your password.

Can I use my computer while it runs?

The game window needs to stay open. It works best when you leave it running on its own.

How do I update?

You don't have to: every time you start it, it checks for a new version and installs it by itself. Your settings stay as they are.
